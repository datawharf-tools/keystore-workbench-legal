# Keystore Workbench: Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-11-13.**

Keystore Workbench is a plugin for JetBrains IDEs published by NoxGrid Systems LLC ("we", "us"), 7901 4th St N, Ste 300, St. Petersburg, FL 33702, USA (Florida document number L26000315407). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

## What the plugin collects

Nothing. This version of the plugin's own code:

- does not collect, store or send any personal data;
- makes no network connections;
- sends no telemetry, usage statistics or crash reports.

Keystores, keys, certificates and passwords you open stay on your computer. We never receive them and cannot recover them.

Licensing is handled by your JetBrains IDE and JetBrains Marketplace, not by the plugin's own code.

## Licensing, payments and the customer data we receive

Trials, subscriptions and payments for the Pro features are handled by JetBrains s.r.o. ("JetBrains") through JetBrains Marketplace and your JetBrains IDE. JetBrains processes your data under its own privacy policy and terms. We receive no payment card details.

**Data we receive.** JetBrains provides plugin vendors with sales and licensing reports. For each transaction they contain: the date, amount, customer code (a unique ID that identifies returning customers), country or region, the organization name (and a personal name only if JetBrains provides it), license type and quantity, license period, reseller and reference number. We also receive any personal data you put in an email to us.

**Source.** JetBrains (sales reports) and you (emails).

**Purpose and legal basis.**
- Managing licenses, answering support requests and handling Marketplace transactions: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- Keeping accounting and tax records: legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).

**Recipients.** Our accountant or tax adviser, our bank and payment providers, and public authorities when the law requires. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

**Storage and transfers.** The data is held in the United States. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, this means a transfer of your data to the United States. We keep it to the minimum above.

**Retention.** Sales and accounting records: for as long as U.S. tax and accounting law requires (currently up to 7 years). Support emails: 2 years after the last contact. After that we delete them.

**Your rights.** You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and you can object to processing. Where the law gives you the right, you can also ask for a copy of your data in a portable format. You can complain to the data protection supervisory authority in the country where you live or work. To use these rights, write to us at the contact below.

## EU representative

We have not appointed a representative in the European Union under GDPR Art. 27, because our processing is occasional, does not involve large-scale special categories of data, and is unlikely to result in a risk to people's rights and freedoms (Art. 27(2)(a)).
