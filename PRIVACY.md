# Keystore Workbench: Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-11-13.**

Keystore Workbench is a plugin for JetBrains IDEs published by NoxGrid Systems LLC, doing business as Datawharf Tools ("we").

## What the plugin collects

Nothing. The plugin:

- does not collect, store or send any personal data;
- makes no network connections;
- sends no telemetry, usage statistics or crash reports.

Keystores, keys, certificates and passwords you open stay on your computer. We never receive them and cannot recover them.

## Licensing and payments

Trials, subscriptions and payments for the Pro features are handled by JetBrains s.r.o. through JetBrains Marketplace and your JetBrains IDE. JetBrains processes that information under its own privacy policy and terms. We may receive the sales and licensing reports JetBrains provides to plugin vendors, and we use them only to manage licenses and our business records.
