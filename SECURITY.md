# Keystore Workbench: Security

## Report a vulnerability

Email **hello@datawharftools.com** with the subject **SECURITY**.

Please include:
- the plugin version and your IDE name and build number;
- what you found and the steps to reproduce it;
- the impact you expect.

Please do not send real private keys or passwords. Test files are enough. Please do not publish details until we have had a chance to fix the problem.

## What to expect

- We confirm receipt within 3 business days.
- We give a first assessment within 10 business days.
- We aim to release a fix for a confirmed vulnerability through JetBrains Marketplace as soon as we can, and tell you when it is out. Fixes are described in the plugin's change notes.

These are targets, not guarantees (the EU reporting deadlines below are legal obligations, not targets). This plugin is built and maintained by a very small team.

## For users in the EU: how we handle serious issues

Under the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847), a software maker must tell the authorities quickly about a vulnerability that is being actively exploited, or about a severe incident. In plain words, this is our process:

1. We read the SECURITY inbox every business day.
2. If a vulnerability in the plugin is being actively exploited, or a severe incident affects the plugin's security, we send an early warning through the EU single reporting platform (run by ENISA, the EU cybersecurity agency) to the responsible national CSIRT within 24 hours of becoming aware of it.
3. We send a fuller notification within 72 hours. We send a final report within 14 days after a fix is available for an exploited vulnerability, or within one month for a severe incident.
4. We tell affected users about the issue and what they can do to protect themselves, through the plugin's change notes and the JetBrains Marketplace page, and directly where we can.
5. We fix the problem, publish a new version on JetBrains Marketplace, and say in the change notes what was fixed.

The plugin's own code makes no network connections and never sends your keystores, keys or passwords anywhere.
